## Mechanics
- One channel at a time per person. Do not hit someone on LinkedIn and email at once. `[tag]`
- Keep touches under four. Six or seven unanswered touches is when people ask to be removed. `[tag]`
- Lead with one specific, checkable thing about them or their company, then one fact about us that connects to it, then one easy ask. `[tag]`
- Never let an agent invent a fact about the recipient or about us. `[tag]`
