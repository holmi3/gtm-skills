# Channels

Defaults; the numbers match `CHANNELS` in `scripts/lint.py`. Change both together.

| Channel | Words | Other limits | Shape | The ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| connection-note | up to 60 | 300 characters, no hyphenated compounds | the hook, one line on why you're writing | a reason to accept, often no question |
| nudge | 6 to 30 | no hyphenated compounds | thanks, then one concrete time (day, clock time, time zone) | the time; never repeat the note's ask |
| dm | 20 to 90 | | hook, one fact about you that connects, ask | one small question |
| inmail | 50 to 100 | subject line | same as dm | one small question |
| cold-email | 25 to 70 | subject of 2 to 6 words | as short as a DM | one small question |
| follow-up | 20 to 60 | | new information or a new reason, never a re-pitch | the same ask, smaller, or a clean close |
| thank-you | 40 to 100 | | one specific thing from the conversation, one next step | |
| info-ask | 50 to 90 | | why them, what you want to learn, how long | 15 minutes, or two questions by message |

## Shape rules for every channel
- Open with the single most specific true thing about the recipient. No warm-up.
- At most one fact about you, from the facts file. Not a résumé.
- One ask, easy to say yes to and easy to ignore without guilt.
- Read the thread first. Never repeat an ask it already makes.
