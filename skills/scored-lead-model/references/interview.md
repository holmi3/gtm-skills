# Interview: 14 questions, one at a time

Start every question with a suggested answer based on the CSV's columns and the answers so far. Show "Question N of 14".

1. **Company.** What company is this for, what do they sell, and to whom? (one sentence plus domain)
2. **Motion.** Inbound or outbound?
3. **Row.** Is one row a person at a company, or a company (account)?
4. **What happens to a scored row?** Suggest bands for the motion. Inbound: A to an AE within 1 hour, B to an SDR, C to nurture, D out. Outbound: A gets a 1:1 sequence, B a segment sequence, C waits for a signal, D out.
5. **Anti-ICP.** Which rows are disqualified no matter what? Suggest: competitors, students, personal email with no company, too small, regions you can't sell to.
6. **Fit (the company).** Which signals make a company a good fit, and which column holds each? Suggest 3 to 5 from the real columns (industry, size band, tech stack, region, business model, work mode).
7. **Persona (the person)** or, for account rows, **prize (size of the win)**: which titles are buyer, influencer, user; or how many people in the target roles, and what share of the company they are.
8. **Intent or awareness (what they did, what they use).** Inbound: demo or contact-sales form, pricing page, content download, a free-text answer naming a pain. Outbound: reply, click, visit, a rival or the category already in use. With no engagement data yet, say so: intent is unknown for now and timing carries the "why now".
9. **Timing (why now).** Which recent events matter? Suggest hiring in the target department, funding in the last 6 to 12 months, a new leader in the buying role, a tech change, a deadline. Ask how recent counts as recent.
10. **Weights.** Propose a maximum per part, totaling 100. Defaults: fit 40, persona 20, intent 25, timing 15. Outbound with no intent data: fit 45, persona 20, intent 0, timing 35. Account scoring: fit 40, prize 25, awareness 15, timing 20.
11. **Points per signal.** For each signal from 6 to 9, propose points inside its part's maximum, as one table.
12. **Cut-offs.** Propose thresholds (default A >= 70, B >= 50, C >= 30, else D; or hot 60, warm 45) and confirm that low-confidence rows never reach the top band.
13. **Eval rows.** Which 2 or 3 rows must land at the top, and which 2 or 3 at the bottom? Real customers or known deals are best.
14. **Never.** Anything the model must never do with this data? (For example: no people data leaves the folder, nothing is pushed to a CRM.)
